Frequently asked questions

Expand all +

What is included in Google AI plans?

There are three Google AI plans that users can subscribe to: Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra.

Can I share the AI benefits included in Google AI plans?

Family plan members on a Google AI Pro plan can enjoy AI benefits and features at no extra cost. Learn more.

I'm a Google Workspace customer. Can I sign up for Google AI plans?

Only personal Google Accounts can sign up for Google AI plans. Workspace customers can sign up for a Gemini add-on to their existing subscription. Learn more about which offering works best for you and your business.

What countries are Google AI plans available in?

You can get started with Google AI plans in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, and 20+ other countries. Visit the Google One website to see a full list of where plans are available.

How do I use the AI benefits included with Google AI plans?

You can access AI benefits like Omni and Gemini in your browser, and in the Gemini, Google Workspace apps, Google Antigravity, and other Google products. To learn more about how to use these features, visit the Google One help centre.

Can I create videos with a Google AI plan?

You can create videos with the Gemini app using the Omni model, available in Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra plans. Visit the Gemini app to learn more.

What is the difference between AI Premium and a Google AI Pro plan?

AI Premium is a Gemini add-on for Google Workspace customers, while Google AI Pro is a Google One plan for personal Google Accounts. Learn more.
